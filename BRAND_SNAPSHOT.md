## 1. Executive Summary & Brand Overview

**Delmela Restaurant & Café** is an established, premium, multi-branch casual-dining and café brand operating across three distinct high-traffic neighborhoods of Addis Ababa: **Bole**, **Sarbet**, and **Summit**.

The brand blends contemporary cosmopolitan culinary trends (artisan superfood bowls, Belgian waffles, savory wraps, handcrafted sandwiches, specialty smoothies) with Ethiopian hospitality and rich coffee culture in architecturally distinct dining atmospheres.

## 3. Brand Identity & Visual Language System

### 🎨 Color Palette
- **Primary Accent (Amber Gold)**: `#D97706` (Warmth, baked golden waffles, artisanal craft)
- **Terracotta Accent (Bole Theme)**: `#C25E3E` (Earthy clay arches, warm Ethiopian pottery)
- **Forest Teal Accent (Summit Theme)**: `#0D9488` (Botanical freshness, lush garden patio)
- **Deep Charcoal Neutral**: `#141311` (Sophisticated text and dark theme backdrops)
- **Warm Cream Background**: `#FBF9F5` (Clean, appetizing, contemporary canvas)
- **Surface Card Background**: `#FFFFFF` (Elevation, crisp borders, tactile hierarchy)

### 🔤 Typography System
- **Display Serif**: `Playfair Display` (600, 700 italic) — *Evokes culinary elegance and classic bistro charm*.
- **Modern Sans**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (400, 500, 600, 700, 800) — *Ultra-clean UI readability on desktop and mobile screens*.
- **Ethiopic Typography**: `Noto Sans Ethiopic` (400, 500, 600, 700, 800) — *Culturally authentic, beautiful Geez glyph rendering*.
